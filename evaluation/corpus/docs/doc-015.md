# Calder & Finch Outfitters, Inc.

## Minutes of the Regular Meeting of the Board of Directors

**Date:** Tuesday, January 20, 2026, 4:00 p.m.
**Place:** Company office, 1045 NW Bond Street, 2nd floor, Bend, Oregon, and by video

**Directors present:** Marguerite Calder (Chair), Dr. Anjali Deshpande-Hall, Rev. Curtis Whitehorse, Yuki Tanabe (CFO and Secretary)
**Absent:** Tobias Finch (CEO), excused
**Also present:** April Sorensen, Sorensen Lyle LLP, outside counsel

### 1. Call to order

Ms. Calder called the meeting to order at 4:04 p.m. and confirmed a quorum under ORS 60.337. The minutes of the November meeting were approved without changes. The meeting followed Robert's Rules informally, as usual.

### 2. CEO leave of absence

The Chair reported that Tobias began chemotherapy on January 6 for non-Hodgkin lymphoma and expects to be away from day-to-day work for at least four months. He asked that staff be told only that he is on medical leave.

**RESOLVED:** Ms. Tanabe is appointed Acting Chief Executive Officer, effective immediately, with an additional stipend of $4,000 per month. Carried 3-0, Ms. Tanabe abstaining.

### 3. Financial report

Ms. Tanabe presented the Q4 results. Revenue was $1.82 million, up 6% on the prior year; the Redmond store again lost money ($41k for the quarter). The line of credit with High Desert Community Bank (loan no. 4410-776-02) stands at $310,000 drawn against a $500,000 limit. The company is in compliance with its covenants.

### 4. Delivery van

**RESOLVED:** The company may buy a used 2023 Ram ProMaster cargo van from Cascade Fleet Sales for $38,450 (VIN 3C6LRVDG5PE518204). It replaces the Sprinter, Oregon plate 482 KVM, which will be sold. Carried unanimously.

### 5. Payroll fraud incident

On December 11, 2025, a payroll clerk responded to a phishing email. The attacker then signed in to the payroll portal from IP address 203.0.113.57 using the Redmond store manager's account (jreyes.redmond) and redirected the direct deposits of two employees. $14,280 was diverted; $9,100 has been recovered. The loss was reported to Summit Ridge Mutual (claim no. CY-25-00817) and to the Bend police (case 2025-00071934). All portal passwords were reset and MFA is now required.

### 6. Executive session: personnel

Ms. Sorensen briefed the Board on the BOLI complaint (no. STEMDP251022-41877) filed by Jesse Reyes, the former Redmond store manager, who alleges he was dismissed in October shortly after telling Tobias he is HIV-positive. Management's position is that Mr. Reyes was dismissed over the cash-handling audit. April recommended settling.

**RESOLVED:** Counsel is authorized to settle the Reyes matter for up to $22,500 plus a neutral reference, on confidential terms. Carried 3-1, Rev. Whitehorse opposed.

### 7. Other business

- Donation of $2,500 to the Three Creeks Trail Alliance approved.
- Bank signers: Tobias is removed temporarily and Dr. Deshpande-Hall is added as second signer on the operating account ending 3382.

### 8. Adjournment

The next regular meeting is set for March 17, 2026. The meeting adjourned at 5:35 p.m.
