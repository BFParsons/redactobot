# Incident Report IR-2026-017

**Company:** Larkspur Benefits Administrators, Inc.
**Prepared by:** Tevita Fonoti, IT Manager, with Granite Peak Managed IT, LLC
**Status:** Contained. **Severity:** P1. **Privileged draft: prepared at the direction of counsel.**
**Tracking no.:** IR-2026-017 / insurer claim FRC-CY-0044186 (Front Range Cyber Mutual)

## Summary

On Monday, October 12, 2026, a claims analyst entered her Microsoft 365 credentials into a fake
SSO page. The attacker used the mailbox for about 31 hours, set up a forwarding rule, and
downloaded one spreadsheet containing enrollment data for **1,204** members of a client plan
(Cedar Hollow School District), including names, dates of birth and Social Security numbers.

## What we know

- Ms. Marsh's password at the time was `Larkspur2026!`, which she also used for the payroll portal. Both changed.
- The attacker never touched the claims system. VPN logs show nothing from her account outside
  the office range (RFC 1918 space behind 198.51.100.14).
- No money moved. Arjun's phone call is the only reason.
- Ms. Marsh was back from medical leave for one week and had missed the September phishing
  training. This is context, not blame. Please keep it out of the all-staff summary.
