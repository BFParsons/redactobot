2. Allegations
(a) That GM failed to tell the company about a conviction for drink-driving at Bradford Magistrates' Court in March 2019 when he applied in 2022, and answered "No" to the convictions question on the application form.
(b) That on 14 October 2025 he drove vehicle YX19 KLO on the Ilkley school contract for 40 minutes beyond his permitted driving time (tachograph card DB04417712300010).

3. GM's response
(a) GM said the ban was 12 months, ended in 2020, and he believed it was "spent". DA referred to the Rehabilitation of Offenders Act 1974. SA said the form asks about any endorsement in the last five years and that the insurer must be told regardless. GM accepted he should have asked. His licence, no. MOXON710153G99LD, now shows no points.
(b) GM said the relief driver did not turn up at Addingham and he could not leave 31 children on the roadside. He rang the depot on 01632 960122 twice and nobody picked up. SA confirmed the call log shows two missed calls from 07700 900164 at 15.52 and 15.58.

4. Mitigation
Mr Ainscough said GM has three years' clean service. He asked the company to take into account that GM was diagnosed with sleep apnoea in the summer and now uses a CPAP machine; DVLA were notified and his D4 medical was passed on 2 September. GM added that his wife left in August and he has the two boys on his own, which is why he had been swapping shifts.

5. Adjournment 10.48 to 11.20.

(b) Not upheld as misconduct. Depot phone cover to be reviewed.
GM's pay is unaffected (£15.90 per hour). The £250 Driver CPC training deduction agreed in July stands.
