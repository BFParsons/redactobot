3. DISTRIBUTIONS MADE IN 2025 - the problem
   The trust instrument lets the trustees make gifts "consistent with the grantor's pattern of giving." The co-trustees paid:
   - Brigid Hargesheimer-Abiola (daughter), $19,000 on 6/2/25. SSN 720-32-3072. Address 4411 Underwood Avenue, Omaha, NE 68132.
   - Felix Nwachi (great-grandson, born 2012), $19,000 into his 529 on 6/2/25, and tuition of $11,450 paid directly to his school. SSN 228-40-1229. Brigid is custodian.
   - Conrad himself, $60,000 on 8/15/25, described in the trustee minutes as "advance on inheritance." SSN ending 5625.
   Under our reading these are gifts by Ottilie (grantor trust), not distributions carrying DNI, so no Schedule K-1 to anyone. Conrad's $60,000 exceeds the annual exclusion: a gift tax return is needed for 2025, and frankly a co-trustee paying himself from an incapacitated parent's trust is something the bank's trust officer should have questioned. Brigid phoned us about it on 11/6, upset; she and Conrad are not speaking.
   If instead the bank is right (complex trust): K-1s to Brigid, Felix and Conrad; the 65-day rule under IRC § 663(b) would be available; and the kiddie tax hits Felix. I have run both ways in the software; difference in total family tax is about $9,800.
