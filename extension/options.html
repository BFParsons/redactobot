Redactobot

A quieter way to protect your pastes

Paste into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini. Clear matches are removed before the text enters the chat box. Uncertain details come to you for review.

The guard follows each site's light or dark appearance. It never sends a message for you.

What to check

Choose what to remove from pasted text. These choices apply to ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini, starting with your next paste. Unchecked categories are left alone.

On-device scanning

Checking the model…

Off: the model loads only when you switch Redactobot on in a chat tab, which can take a minute or two after a break. On: Chrome loads it in the background at startup, so switching on is quick. No text is read or sent either way.

Show the Redactobot switch on

The switch sits under the chat box and starts off each time a page loads. Switch it on to check pastes in that tab.

Second privacy pass

Cloud cleanup requires All categories. Narrower selections stay on this device. When enabled with all checks, a completed local scan sends identity-scrubbed text through Firebase to Gemini to remove remaining sensitive facts. Identifying uncertainty pauses for review. This cannot guarantee anonymity.

The connection test uses all categories and a fixed fictional sample, regardless of your paste settings.

What this version covers

Text pasted into recognized message editors. Typing, file uploads and image pastes are not scanned. Review important material yourself; automatic detection can miss details.

Pending pastes and identity mappings are held in extension memory and cleared when the paste finishes, is canceled, or expires. Only your preferences are saved. Chrome may download the AI model during setup.

Redactobot is a technology demonstration, not a validated compliance tool. Do not paste real sensitive data. Privacy policy and terms of use
